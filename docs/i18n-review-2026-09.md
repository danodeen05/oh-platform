# Translation review, September 2026 (Task F1)

For native review: every customer-site message key that Task F1 added or changed, with all four locales side by side, then the database copy the backfill writes. Baseline: `96abcf7`. The business plan (`plan.*`) is out of scope.

Conventions used throughout:

- zh-TW is written for Taiwan and zh-CN for the mainland. They are separate translations, not a character conversion (for example 點數 and 积分, 簡訊 and 短信, 報到 and 签到).
- A pod is 包廂 (zh-TW), 包厢 (zh-CN) and cabina (es) everywhere on the customer site. Before F1 the site mixed 包廂 with 座艙 and "pod".
- Kept in English on purpose (the allowlist in `apps/web/lib/site/i18n-allowlist.ts`): brand and product names, the two mall names (Chinese copy names them in Chinese with the English in parentheses, e.g. 城溪購物中心（City Creek Center）, the same convention as the Location rows; the English is what a reader types into a map app), the SMS keywords STOP and HELP (carriers act on those exact words), email addresses, and pod codes like B-07.
- USDA Prime reads 美國農業部極佳級 / 美国农业部极佳级 in Chinese; Spanish keeps "USDA Prime", the usual US Spanish label.
- Plan voice: short, declarative, calm. No em dashes, no emoji.
